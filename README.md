# Build your own lesson pathway

Pick a concept from your subject, choose creative methods, arrange them into a pathway, then reflect on your choices.

## 1. Your details

So we can connect your pathway to you.

Name Parent subject / subject you can teach Concept or topic Class / level

## 2. Lesson components

Each box is a method of art-based pedagogy. Tap **Add** on the ones that fit your concept. Use as few or as many as you need, then design the lesson through them.

## 3. Your lesson pathway

Arrange the order in which learners will meet your chosen methods, and note what happens in each step. You can also attach your own image, video, audio or animation to any step.

## 4. Reflect

Answer in your own words. There are no right or wrong answers.
